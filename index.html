The story of a legend

This is not a made-up mascot. Budi is a real orangutan, and his story is wilder than any chart.

2014: Starting from zero

Life in a chicken cage

Budi spent the first year of his life in a chicken coop, and his entire diet was condensed milk. Yes, literally: condensed milk for breakfast, lunch and dinner. His portfolio was deep in the red, but even back then he was a world-class hodler.

December 2014: The Rescue

10 hours of pain and hope

The International Animal Rescue team carried him to the rescue centre, more than 10 hours by boat and by road. Budi was so weak he couldn't even sit up. The vets weren't sure he would make it. He made it. Remember this moment: that was the bottom the pump started from.

2015-2016: Rehabilitation

Learning to chew, learning to fly

Budi had to relearn everything: chewing solid food, sitting up, climbing branches. The first thing he did was stuff himself with oranges and bananas (leafy greens were politely ignored, the ape knows what's good). Then came best friends Jemmi and Jacko, the first gang and the first community.

2017-2022: School of life

Baby School → Forest School

Budi graduated from Baby School and enrolled in Forest School, the Ivy League for future jungle kings. He learned to build nests, fish termites out of rotten branches, and became so popular that he now teaches the younger apes himself. From chicken cage to mentorship. Now that's growth.

June 2023: The Release

Freedom. Full bags. Moon.

After 8 years of rehabilitation, Budi was released into the wild at Bukit Baka Bukit Raya National Park in Borneo. Four days of trekking through the jungle, and he was home. Free, healthy and unstoppable. Which is exactly where $BUDI is heading.
